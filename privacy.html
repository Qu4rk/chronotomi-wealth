Privacy & Discretion Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

1. Introduction

At Chronotomi Wealth LTD ("we," "us," or "our"), headquartered in Cyprus, we understand that confidentiality is the cornerstone of our relationship with our clients. This Privacy & Discretion Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you visit our website or engage our luxury asset and wealth advisory services.

2. Data Collection

We collect information strictly necessary to provide our bespoke services. This includes:

  • Identity & Contact Data: Names, email addresses, and phone numbers provided via our inquiry and consultation forms.
  • Financial Inquiries: Details regarding your wealth advisory needs, asset acquisition requests, and investment objectives.
  • Technical Data: IP addresses and browser types collected temporarily to ensure website security and functionality.

3. Purpose of Processing

Your data is processed exclusively to:

  • Facilitate direct, confidential communications regarding your inquiries.
  • Source and secure luxury timepieces or alternative assets on your behalf.
  • Provide tailored wealth advisory and strategic planning services.
  • Comply with applicable legal, anti-money laundering (AML), and regulatory obligations in Cyprus and the European Union.

4. Data Protection & Confidentiality

We employ rigorous technical and organizational measures to ensure your data remains secure. Access to your personal and financial information is strictly limited to authorized advisory partners who are bound by non-disclosure and confidentiality obligations.

5. Your Rights (GDPR)

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the right to request access to, correction of, or erasure of your personal data. You may also object to processing or request data portability. To exercise these rights, please contact our privacy compliance team securely.

6. Contact Us

For any inquiries regarding this policy or to exercise your rights, please contact us at info@chronotomi.com.
